Creator Tips
⏰
Best time to post on Instagram
Tuesday to Friday, 9am–11am or 7pm–9pm. Avoid Monday mornings and Sunday nights.
🎯
Hook = first 3 seconds
If your reel doesn't grab in 3 seconds, people scroll. Always start with a strong line.
📌
Save rate matters more than likes
Content people save tells the algorithm it's valuable. Make content worth saving.
🔁
Consistency beats perfection
Posting 3x a week consistently beats posting 10 times one week and then going silent.
